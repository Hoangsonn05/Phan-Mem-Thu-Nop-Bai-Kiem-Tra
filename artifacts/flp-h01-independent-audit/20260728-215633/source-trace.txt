3. Thread safety:
   Yes for the exercised access: Interlocked.Increment at line 246 and Volatile.Read at line 77.

4. Increment owner:
   SuccessfulPushCloud.PushAsync increments exactly once at method entry.

5. Semantics:
   One increment per attempted ICloudAdapter.PushAsync call, hence per eligible outbox item attempt.
   It is not per wake, batch, entity table, or successful result.
   A retry would increment it again.

6. Business meaning of PushCalls == 1:
   None is encoded. It appears intended as evidence that the wake occurred, but it is an aggregate transport-attempt counter.

   No. The business requirement is one logical exam_sessions projection becoming Synced/ShareReady.

8. Does it poll a generic counter?
   Yes, before polling the persisted target entity state.

9. Can polling miss 1?
   Yes. The worker processes all 3 completed fake pushes rapidly; 10 ms polling sometimes sees 1 and sometimes sees final 3.

15. Can create emit two wake signals?
